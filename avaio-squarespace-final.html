The Situation

A serious team with a
startup problem

AVAIO Digital is a lean, highly experienced private equity–backed developer of hyperscale data center campuses across North America and Europe. The team was actively advancing major campuses—seasoned experts in finance, engineering, and energy—making real progress on significant developments.

The problem: their brand and website didn't reflect any of that. A rushed identity blended into a crowded field. Poor mobile experience, broken project details, and an overall impression more startup than operator. Meanwhile, more eyes were landing on AVAIO every day—hyperscalers evaluating sites, investors doing diligence, communities researching campuses.

  • A vanilla, forgettable brand aesthetic that blended into the competitive landscape

  • Poor user experience, especially on mobile where many initial visits occur

  • Campus and project details that were hard to find or broken, undermining credibility

  • An overall impression of a startup still figuring things out, rather than a disciplined, well-capitalised developer

Brand in context — out-of-home advertising system

"The external story needed to catch up to the agility of the team and their real, emphasis on real, pipeline."

What We Did

Five phases, one
coherent system

We rebuilt the brand from the inside out—starting with deep discovery and competitive research, and ending with a live Webflow site that gives AVAIO room to grow without bottlenecks.

01
Competitive Research & Personas

We benchmarked leading data center developers, mapped the visual and verbal tropes in the category, and identified real whitespace. Then we built personas for the three audiences most likely to interact with AVAIO—hyperscaler site selection teams, institutional investors, and local community officials—which became a filter for every subsequent decision.

Competitive analysis Persona development Category mapping
02
Brand Strategy & Positioning

Through in-depth interviews with leadership across finance, engineering, energy, and development, we uncovered what was genuinely different about AVAIO. We built a comprehensive brand platform: mission, vision, purpose, positioning, values, and audience-specific value propositions. The strategy moved AVAIO from "another new developer" to a trusted, credible, and sharp operator.

Brand platform Positioning Messaging Value propositions
03
Visual Identity & Design System

With strategy in place, we translated it into a visual system that signals maturity and technical confidence. A new logo and mark designed to flex across digital, print, and site signage. A supporting system of typography, colour, and layout patterns suitable for institutional and technical audiences—modern and distinctive without being trendy or over-designed.

Logo & mark Typography Colour system Collateral
04
Website Architecture, Content & UX

We rethought the digital presence from the ground up—content outline, sitemap, base copy tuned for each audience, and UX design concepts that resolved issues with campus visibility, mobile experience, and navigation. The website is now a credible extension of what stakeholders hear in meetings, instead of a liability.

UX design Content strategy Copywriting Information architecture
05
Webflow Development & Lead Capture

The site was built on Webflow — giving AVAIO's team the ability to update content, add campuses, and avoid reliance on external developers. Improved lead capture on key pages means high-intent visitors can now contact AVAIO easily. Fully responsive, with special attention paid to mobile usability where many initial visits occur.

Webflow development CMS setup Lead capture Mobile optimisation
Visual Identity

A system built to
scale with ambition

The identity is flexible enough to carry AVAIO from a development-stage pitch to a fully operational campus—from investor decks to community town halls, letterhead to site signage.

The Aquilla constellation in the logo mark connects with the immense responsibility of AVAIO as the facilitators of innovation and societal progress. The tagline of "Constellations of Capacity" has multiple meanings, but leans into nodes of capacity connected across a network, scalable by design.
